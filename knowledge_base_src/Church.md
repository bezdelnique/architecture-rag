The Church is a location in the video game The Matrix: Path of Neo during the mission The Security Guard.

## The Mission

During the beginning of the mission the player learns the story of The Security Guard. He was originally a police officer but after seeing Neo die then come back running along walls and in combat he retired. After that he worked as a security guard in the church.

Neo appears to save the potential but he runs away and Neo has to save him from an Agent.
