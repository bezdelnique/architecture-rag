## Biography

When the Cypherite liaison known as Recant retired from the organization, he would only agree to come back to the organization if one of the members were able to solve his series of tasks. After she solved Recant's riddles, she was rewarded with choosing Recant's fate. Deeming him to be a valuable asset, she re-invited him back to his former organization.
