Alsos was a Zionite liaison aboard the Logos II in the Vector instance of The Matrix Online.

## Biography

James Keenan was awakened at regulation age before the truce. he witnessed the events of the truce within the walls of Zion. Although he does not yet have enough experience in general, this is all overcompensated by his knowledge of the Real, which is more than most newly awakened Redpills. Despite his short age, he is shown to be highly skilled in communication, which had led him to become an efficient carrier within Zion.
