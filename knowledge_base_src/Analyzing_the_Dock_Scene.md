## Other Pictures

Dock Pic #1

So we see Kid running over to greet Neo when the Neb arrives at the Dock. I can assume that these three ships from left to right are the Novalis, Vishnu, and the Ganesha. How? Well, the walkway that Kid is running on is at a 90% angle to the Neb's landing platform when Neo comes out of the ship (since the opening cargo door is facing forwards.) Therefore, the three ships are on landing pads 4, 5, and 6. That means they are the Novalis, Vishnu, and the Ganesha.

Dock Pic #2

We get to see a ship we never get to see again – the Icarus. The ships from left to right are the Mjolnir, Logos, and the Icarus. I know the one on the left is the Mjolnir, since we get plenty of beauty shots of her. The middle one is definitely the Logos, and the one on the right is the Icarus, based off of this sketch by the design team of The Matrix Reloaded – it has the shape of the Icarus sketch.
