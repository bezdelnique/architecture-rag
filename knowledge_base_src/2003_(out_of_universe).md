The Matrix Revolutions poster

2003 was the year that The Matrix Revolutions was released, along with a number of other works in The Matrix franchise.

## Official releases

May 14: Enter the Matrix (North America)
May 15: Enter the Matrix (Europe)
May 15: The Matrix Reloaded
June 3: The Animatrix
June 19: Enter the Matrix (Japan)
October 17: The Matrix Revolutions (Premiere: Los Angeles, California)
November 5: The Matrix Revolutions (General release: United States)
November 21: The Matrix Comics Volume 1
