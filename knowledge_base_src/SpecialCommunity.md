From the community

The Matrix Wiki is a fan-written encyclopedia that anyone can edit. As such, it will always belong to the community and will always be open to anyone.

Wish to join? Then please start by reading the following:

Register

While you can edit anonymously, a free account allows you to sign in and create a username. It is also an easy way to keep track of your own contributions and will help you communicate with the rest of the community.

Looking for help?

Check out the list of helpful links to the right or visit Fandom's Community wiki for a list of help pages. You may also directly ask any of the active Administrators through their talk pages.

Concerned about making mistakes?

Please read the Matrix Wiki Manual of Style to get acquainted with the wiki's rules on writing. You can also visit any of the already existing articles for reference on what to do.

In any case, be bold.

Go for it and start editing. If you do not feel like making any big changes then feel free to just start fixing any of the small errors you encounter.

Start a new article.

Type the title of your article in the box above and then click the "Create page" button to begin writing articles.

Join our community discussions to start talking with other fans.

Also, please check out the Administrators' noticeboard to suggest changes to the Wiki.

Don't know where to start?

The cards shown near the top of this page shows plenty of good starting places if you wish to add content. But you can also check our other "TO DO" lists below:
Merge articles that need merging – Sometimes, two users get the same idea for an article, but put them on pages with different names. When we find them, we tag them to be merged, in hopes someone will combine the two into a single, cohesive article. Don't forget to remove the merge tags when you're done!
Create redlink articles – Many articles reference topics that haven't been addressed in the Matrix Wiki yet, creating redlinks. Some reference many such topics; these articles are less useful to our users, because much of the related information they might want to seek is unavailable. It is helpful to create new articles to fill in the gaps of these articles with an excess of redlinks; please don't forget to remove the redlink tag from the original article once the majority of the links have been filled in.
Create a wanted page – Some topics are referenced by a great many pages, but haven't had an article created about them yet.
Create a requested page – Users sometimes request the creation of a particular article. You can help by creating one for them.
Fix double redirects – Sometimes, an article redirects to another article, which in turn redirects to yet another article. When this happens, instead of smoothly being redirected, a user must instead manually click through to the third page. In order to prevent this, we try to eliminate double redirects, pointing all redirects at their final destination.
Categorize a page – Categories assist in easy navigation by grouping similar topics together; please assign a category to these articles.
Expand a stub - Some articles are merely placeholders, waiting for an intrepid contributor like yourself to come along and expand upon them.
Expand a short page – Some of these articles might use some more content.
Help proofread articles – Some articles may have content that needs to be cleaned-up.
