Alexa was a young woman working for a small group of the Resistance that uses the Matriculation program to convert captured Machines to fight along their side.

## Biography

Operating as part of a small group that trapped and converted Machines into fighting for the Resistance, Alexa scouted the surface for possible Machines to target. After identifying a target, she then lured the Machine through underground tunnels and passageways into the group's hidden outpost where they ambushed and disabled the victims with the aid of other converted Machines.

During her last and final mission, Alexa was scouting the surface with Baby when Baby spotted a pair of Runners. Alexa then lured the Runners into their hideout but not before one of the Runners had covertly deployed a beacon. The beacon then allowed Sentinels to track down the hidden outpost resulting in Alexa's entire group getting killed.

In her last moments however, Alexa turned to the very Runner which was the sole survivor of their earlier ambush. The Runner, having lost its partner, had already chosen to convert after developing affections for Alexa and so managed to rescue her from the metal claws of the Machine attackers. With Alexa wounded and unconscious, the Machine then jacked both of them into the Matriculation program and offered Alexa partnership. But Alexa's mind was unable to handle the stress and shock of the proposed reality and instinctively rejected the program, perishing in the process.
