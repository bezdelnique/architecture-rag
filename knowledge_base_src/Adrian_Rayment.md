## Adrian Rayment (left)

### Born

May 14, 1970 (age 55)

### Occupations

ActorMartial Artist

### Matrix role

The Twins

### Other works

Intergalactic CombatDoctors

Adrian Rayment and his twin brother Neil each played one of The Twins, who were henchmen of The Merovingian in The Matrix Reloaded.

The brothers were carpenters, whom the producers of The Matrix' original trilogy decided to cast, because they fit the profile of being identical twins and capable of martial arts stunts.
