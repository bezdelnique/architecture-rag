Carlos was a Bluepill who worked with Jimmy Scarvone and Tony for the crimelord Kaplan.

## History

Tony and Carlos went with Jimmy to betray their boss, but Kaplan was already there waiting for them. The boss warned Jimmy and told him to get "Carlitos to put his little gun away", but Carlos became nervous, refusing and opening fire, killing one of Kaplan's men before being shot himself.
