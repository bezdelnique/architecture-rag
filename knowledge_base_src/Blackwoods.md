The Blackwood gang was an Exile gang located in Mara and Magog that have named themselves after the dreaded prison construct with almost the same name. They resided in Mara and were led by Owl Bangheart. In Chapter 9.3.5, the gang suffered from the code overrides that were generated by the Oligarch known as Halborn.
