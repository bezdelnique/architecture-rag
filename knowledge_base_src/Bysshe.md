Bysshe (properly known as a "Research Program") was a Machine program, like Wollstonecraft, who was designed to check and investigate anomalous humanoid exiles. Both exiles were encountered and seen during the event concerning the hunt for Invalesco.

"Bysshe" was the middle name of "Percy Bysshe Shelley", one of the most famous english poets.
