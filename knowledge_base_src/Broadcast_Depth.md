Broadcast Depth was a comic story written and drawn by Bill Sienkiewicz for The Matrix Comics Series 2 which was included with corrections in The Matrix Comics Volume 2. It was later republished in The Matrix Comics: 20th Anniversary Edition.

## Story

During an Zionite operation of finding potentials, two sisters are looking for their mother as a birthday present. However, their ships were detected by Sentinels and all of the people that jacked in were killed.
