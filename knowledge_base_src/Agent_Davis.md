Agent Davis was an undercover Agent in the comic Get It? who was posing as James, a friend to the human Johnny. Agents Davis and Lewis watched The Matrix movie with Johnny and, after determining how little he really knew of the truth, allowed him to continue living his life, ignorant of his friends true identities.
