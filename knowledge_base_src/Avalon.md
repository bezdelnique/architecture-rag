Avalon is a female member of the resistance.

Five years after she was freed from the Matrix, Avalon and her crew was on their way to a disinfection mission: to track down and destroy a virus contained in the virtual money load inside the Matrix.

The operation went on with bikes, armed with guns and grenades. As they approached the money load, they interfered with a gang led by Boss-man that wanted to rob the infected money.

The robbery attempt alerted an Agent who entered the bank and slaughtered all of the gang, as well as all the crew members but Avalon. Before the Agent could kill Avalon and Boss, she threw a hand grenade disabling the Agent.

At a nearby extraction point, Avalon revealed bits and pieces about the real danger, the Matrix, to Boss. Avalon escaped through the phone while Boss was gunned down by incoming police forces.
