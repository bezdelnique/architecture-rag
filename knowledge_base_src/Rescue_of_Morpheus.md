## Rescue of Morpheus

### Conflict

Machine War

### Objective

To rescue Morpheus from the Agents

### Date

c.2699, late stages of the Machine War[1]

### Location

Mega City, the Matrix

### Outcome

Morpheus is rescuedNeo begins to believe he is The One

The rescue of Morpheus was a rescue mission made by Neo and Trinity to rescue Morpheus from Agents Smith, Jones, and Brown in the Matrix during the Machine War.

## Prelude

The Nebuchadnezzar operatives are ambushed at Lafayette Hotel, their previous base of operations, after Cypher betrays its location to Agent Smith. As a result, Morpheus is captured and only Neo and Trinity survive to return to the Real World.

### Agent Smith's Desire

Agent Brown about to inject Morpheus with truth serum while Agent Smith lectures

Agent Smith tells Morpheus his reason for trying to destroy Zion

After capturing Morpheus, Agents Smith, Jones, and Brown take him to the near-top floor of a heavily guarded, twenty-story building where they connect him to a machine and inject him with truth serum. Agent Smith also lectures Morpheus about early versions of the Matrix that were designed to be a paradise, which humans universally refused to accept, and waxes philosophical about the nature of humanity being identical to a virus that systemically used up the planet's natural resources. Their goal in this was to break Morpheus' will and get him to divulge the Zion mainframe access codes, which would allow the Agents to enter Zion and destroy the Resistance. However, Morpheus did not break.

Frustrated with his resilience, Agent Smith ordered the others to leave him alone with Morpheus. He took off his earpiece and sunglasses and confided in Morpheus his true feelings about the Matrix; he hated being trapped within it and felt that being forced to live among the humans was 'infecting' him. He made a personal, desperate appeal and threat to Morpheus, to give him the Zion access codes so he could enter Zion and destroy the Resistance, as without them there would be no need for him with remain in the Matrix and he could be freed.

Morpheus, suffocating and with his eyes already turning white, hears Agent Smith but still wills himself to remain silent.

### The Plan

Neo and Trinity prepares for the rescue mission

While the interrogation is carried out by the Agents, Tank monitors Morpheus' physical body in the Nebuchadnezzar. Seeing telltale signs that Morpheus is suffering from torture, Tank decides that Morpheus must be unplugged before he eventually breaks and gives out information to the Machines.

Neo and Trinity within a Construct loaded with weaponry

Before Tank can pull the plug however, Neo stops him and instead shares how this future was already imparted to him by The Oracle. Neo then tells Tank and Trinity that he is going back to the Matrix to rescue Morpheus, declaring how he believes he can still save their Captain. Trinity likewise declares to join Neo and help alongside him.

Jacking into a construct, Neo asks Tank for weapons and supplies which the operator readily loads into their sandbox. And after taking a Heckler & Koch MP5K from the firearms rack, Neo gives Trinity a reassuring look that the mission will work.

## The Mission

### Lobby Shootout

Neo opening his coat to display some "metal"

The crew tracks down Morpheus to a heavily secured building and Neo is the first to enter and goes through a security check, placing a zipped-up duffel bag on a scanner's conveyor belt and walking through a metal detector that immediately sounds off an alarm. One of the security guards approach Neo to do further inspections when Neo opens up his coat to reveal a variety of pistols and sub-machine guns attached to his body by belts, holsters, and slings. Looking up at Neo, the guard could only curse before Neo knocks him down with a palm strike.

Neo attacking with a palm strike

Neo immediately unholsters two MP5k sub-machine guns and proceeds to shoot at three of the security guards, killing two on his left side and one on his right. One other guard quickly takes cover, hiding behind a nearby pillar and calling for backup.

Herself backing up Neo, Trinity enters the building wielding a Micro Uzi sub-machine gun and empties her clip at the surviving security guard to neutralize him. She then discards the empty gun and picks up Neo's duffel bag and the pair walk down the lobby towards the elevators. Neo discards his own guns in favor of equipping dual M9 pistols from his waist.

Not even a few steps in, a unit of soldiers armed with M16 assault rifles and SPAS-12 shotguns enter the lobby from the sides of the elevators. They then aim their guns at Neo and Trinity and order them to stop. The pair, briefly looking at each other, runs to opposite sides of the lobby. Trinity drops the duffel bug and runs to the left of the guards while Neo runs to the right. This immediately starts a shootout.

Neo takes cover from the incoming fire

Neo empties his pistols while shooting back and dashing and slamming to the wall. Quickly, he retreats back to a nearby heavy pillar and takes shelter from the gunfire being rained on him. He then takes out two Skorpion vz. 61 sub-machine guns and waits for a lull in the onslaught.

Trinity runs on the wall to avoid the rain of fire

On the opposite side, Trinity is quick to reach the walls while moving forwards toward the elevators, avoiding the curtain of fire from the enemy tactical forces by using the pillars as cover for her advance. Two soldiers suddenly reveal themselves in front of Trinity and shoot their assault rifles at her. Trinity, in turn, runs to the walls and on them, escaping the enemies' immediate line-of-fire by jumping off the wall and flipping in a cartwheel-like motion to land behind one of the pillars.

One of the soldiers nearing Trinity reload and Trinity quickly moves in from an angle that obscures herself from the other soldiers' views. She then kicks away his rifle followed immediately by a solid snap kick to the soldier's chest and a roundhouse to the neck that knocks the soldier out. She then quickly runs back for cover at the next column as the other soldiers resume their fire on her.

Neo charges up to the guards while firing his sub-machine guns

After performing a front snap kick on the shotgun, the weapon flips for Trinity to take

After detecting the slightest pause, Neo charges forward while firing full salvo. Immediately, he kills two of the three soldiers in his way. This sudden advancement attracts the attention of all the soldiers currently trained on Trinity and they all begin shooting at Neo instead. Not missing the chance, Trinity rushes forward to the back of one of the soldiers and kicks up his SPAS 12 pump-action shotgun spinning in the air before promptly taking it and firing it at the soldier's back. In a rapid follow through, she also shoots down the next soldier gunning for Neo. She then takes cover and heads back.

Five more soldiers are stationed at the last row of columns next to the elevators and they keep firing at Neo as he charges forward. Exhausting the ammunition for his sub-machine guns, Neo discards them without stopping his momentum, choosing to angle towards one of the fallen soldiers lying between him and the elevators instead. He then performs a cartwheel to dodge the enemies' continued stream of bullets while also using the chance to pick from the ground the dead soldier's M16 assault rifle.

Neo kicks the final guard

Armed with the assault rifle, Neo continues to perform a one-handed cartwheel all the while firing with his other arm and killing three of the soldiers. After landing, he immediately hides behind a nearby pillar and takes the chance to pull out two Micro Uzis from his back before marching towards the remaining two shooters and holding them down with an alternating downpour of bullets and managing to kill one of the remaining shooters who mistimes his assault on Neo. Once again empty, Neo discards his guns just as the last soldier comes out and aims at Neo. With a quick reaction, he jumps at the final shooter and lands an aerial roundhouse kick to the soldier's face, knocking him out.

With all the soldiers down, Trinity retrieves the duffel bag and the pair regroups to finally enter one of the elevators. As they go up to the roof, the elevator doors close on a ruined and seemingly lifeless lobby.

### Elevator Detonator

Neo and Trinity using an elevator cable to ascend to the roof

Trinity unzips the bag and takes out a huge explosive device which she wires to the elevator floor. Neo meanwhile stops the elevator and opens the ceiling hatch to climb to the roof and clamp unto one end of the elevator cables. Neo and Trinity then secure each other to it and Neo, before shooting the cable that detaches it from the elevator cart, recites that "there is no spoon". The solid metal box just plummets to the ground as one of the cables holding it steady is cut and it pulls the the remaining cable down with it, yanking Neo and Trinity at the other end towards the roof. Just a moment later, a large explosion comes from the bottom of the elevator shaft and the explosion engulfs the lobby.

### Rooftop Rumble

The pair of Resistance operatives arrive on the roof and immediately cause havoc to the security stationed there. One of the soldiers sitting behind the cockpit of a B-212 helicopter contacts the Agents via radio and informs them that the roof is under attack.

Neo and Trinity fight more guards on the rooftop

While each hand holds on to the barrels of two M16 assault rifles, Neo fights off the soldiers holding the guns while defensively trying to prevent the weapons from being fired. Neo strikes the soldier to his right with his left knee and, with the same leg, kicks the left soldier's side. Neo repeats this sequence of attacking once more until the soldier on his left, with his gun, is knocked to the ground. Neo then forcefully takes the remaining opponent's assault rifle, striking him in the chin with it and pushing him back before hurriedly turning around to find Trinity.

Meanwhile, Trinity was fighting off one of the other guards whom she eventually knocks out with a series of chops and elbows. Quickly, another soldier sneaks behind her with an assault rifle but is shot and killed by Neo just in time when Neo turns around.

However, the guard pushed back by Neo recovers and picks up his comrade's assault rifle while Neo was busy facing away. Fortunately, Trinity is quick to hurl her knife at the soldier, piercing his head through his helmet.

Instantly, the pilot staying at the helicopter cockpit is possessed by Agent Jones and exits the helicopter. Trinity first notices and then Neo. Turning around, Neo pulls out two M9 pistols and fires all his bullets at Agent Jones.

Neo dodges Agent Jones' bullets

Agent Jones dodges all the bullets perfectly without moving a step and draws out his own weapon, aiming it at Neo. Neo asks Trinity for help just as the Agent begins shooting. Neo bends back and manages to avoid sustaining any fatal wounds by twisting and turning his body and arms while ducking back. Unfortunately, he is still grazed near his shoulder and on his shin, making him drop flat on his back.

The next moment, Agent Jones is standing next to Neo pointing his weapon at him while declaring how Neo was "only human". Before the Agent could shoot however, Trinity manages to sneak past him and, touching her gun's barrel unto his left temple, shoots Agent Jones in the face while daring him to dodge. The bullet kills the Agent and he disappears, leaving behind the corpse of the Bluepill pilot that has reverted back to its original self image.

Surveying the roof and eyeing the helicopter, Trinity calls Tank and asks him to upload unto her the knowledge to pilot a B-212 helicopter.

### Helicopter Shootout

Neo uses the B-212 helicopter's mounted minigun to fire at Agents Smith, Jones, and Brown

Agent Jones returns to the interrogation room, now flooded by the emergency fire sprinklers, just as the helicopter loudly comes into view outside the windows and carefully turns to its side to reveal Neo behind a mounted minigun.
The Agents all equip their firearms and fire at Neo just as Neo rains down a torrent of bullets that destroys the windows and kills Agent Brown, then Agent Jones, and finally, Agent Smith.

After all the Agents disappear, Morpheus regains consciousness and, upon realizing that he is being rescued, forces himself free from his bindings and begins running towards the helicopter. Meanwhile, the Agents immediately possesses the nearby guards and proceed to return via the anteroom. And upon seeing Morpheus running, Agent Smith quickly begins shooting at him while carefully aiming for his legs, even shooting through the walls with zero visibility but with such accuracy that one of his bullets manages to hit Morpheus just below the knee.

Neo jumps off the helicopter to catch Morpheus

Morpheus slows down before his jump and Neo, reacting to the situation, readily attaches himself to a harness secured to the helicopter interior via a chain and jumps off to catch Morpheus midair. The two men then hang tight as Trinity attempts to ascend to safety. Agent Smith, now positioned by the broken windows, shoots at the helicopter's fuel tanks just in time to cause enough damage and ensure that the helicopter will eventually come crashing back down. Trinity, in an attempt to give Neo and Morpheus the chance to safely escape the eventual crash, maneuvers the helicopter towards several building rooftops and hovers low.

Trinity swings off the helicopter from chains attached to Neo

Morpheus and Neo each let go when the chance comes and lands to relative safety. Neo, landing while still harnessed to the helicopter via the chain, braces himself as an anchor against the rooftop and against certain death just as the helicopter comes hurtling down towards the side of the adjacent building, dragging Neo with it. No longer able to pilot the helicopter as it nosedives, Trinity quickly gets up from the pilot seat and grabs hold of the chains. And with a carefully placed shot of her pistol, detaches the chains, yanking her out of the helicopter while Neo manages to keep himself planted to the edge of the roof. Trinity swings into the glass windows below Neo and the helicopter crashes into the next building, warping its walls into an explosion of broken glass before the helicopter itself explodes in a fiery ball of fire.

Neo reels Trinity up to his side and they take a moment to gaze at each other. Morpheus, coming up to them, restates his belief in Neo.

## Aftermath

Trinity and Neo after a death-defying ordeal to rescue Morpheus

Neo and Agent Smith (pictured from behind) as they prepare for their first duel

While on the rooftop, Morpheus approached Trinity and asks her if she now fully believes that Neo is The One. Neo however attempts to protest this with what The Oracle has told him. But Morpheus cuts him off, telling him that The Oracle's message to him was meant for him alone and that, while it was what he needed to hear, he would soon learn the truth. They then contact Tank and Morpheus personally asks him for an exit which the operator gladly provides.

Afterwards, Agents Smith, Jones, and Brown inspect the now empty building rooftops. Agent Brown then informs the others that they have the location of the Nebuchadnezzar and that Sentinels are on their way to destroy it. Agent Smith however looks out unto the city and refuses to give up search for the operatives while they still remain inside the Matrix.

Eventually, Agent Smith tracks them down to their exit point at a subway station and, while failing to stop Morpheus and Trinity from leaving the Matrix, manages to destroy their hardline in time to prevent Neo from leaving.
