Acavaria was a female member of E Pluribus Neo who worked as a liaison officer and a crewmember of Neo's Hope in the Vector instance of The Matrix Online.

## Biography

Dani was a orphan at a very young age, and so had very little or no memory of her parents. She had always known that there was something wrong with the world she lived in. When she was finally awakened when she was fourteen, she took her training very seriously. She shunned the simulation for several years and in that time learned what it meant to be human. She, however, returned into the Matrix at the request of Kid.
