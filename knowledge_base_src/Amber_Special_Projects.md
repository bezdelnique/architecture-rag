Captain Sinclair: head of ASP

Amber Special Projects was the name of an organization controlled by the Spectrum Sibling known as Amber, security force dressed in yellow caps and jumpsuits dedicated to ensuring her safety. Each member had a special A.S.P. ID card. They were headed by Captain Sinclair. They normally reside in the neighborhood called Rogers Way.
