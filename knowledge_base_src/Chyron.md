Chyron was a Resistance fighter who worked towards the successful conversion of Machines into fighting for the side of the Resistance. He, like the rest of his teammates, was killed in the midst of a conversion operation when one of the Machines they ambushed was able to reveal and transmit the hidden location of their base; resulting in the base getting attacked by the Machines which checked on the transmitted location.

## Biography

Chyron was a redpill operator for his crew who carefully coordinated the luring, trapping, and ambushing of Machines back into their secret base. Machines that survived were then later given the choice of conversion, or of befriending humanity, through a Matriculation program.

After Alexa successfully lured a pair of Runners back into their base, one of the Machines clandestinely dropped a beacon which guided the rest of the Machines into the base. The Runners were eventually ambushed, with one of the pair getting destroyed while the other was paralyzed.

Chyron then initiated the conversion process, jacking in the surviving Runner, himself, Alexa, and few other crew members into the Matriculation program. Midway during the course of the program, however, the Machines responded with an attack.

Chyron immediately plugged himself out of the program and grabbed a lightning rifle, momentarily helping to defend the base and saving Raul before everyone is ultimately killed by the attacking Machines.
