## Bruce Spence

### Born

September 17, 1945 (age 80)

### Occupations

Actor

### Matrix role

The Trainman

### Other works

The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King, Star Wars Episode III: Revenge of the Sith, Mad Max 2, Ace Ventura: When Nature Calls.

Bruce Spence is a New Zealand character actor primarily working in Australia, whose career spans over 3 decades playing characters in countless sci-fi, fantasy and action-adventure films.

In The Matrix franchise, he played the role of the exile program called The Trainman who made appearances in The Matrix Revolutions, The Matrix Online, The Matrix: Path of Neo, and Enter the Matrix.
