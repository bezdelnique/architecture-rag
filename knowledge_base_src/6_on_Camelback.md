Motel 6 in real life, as of 2011

Motel 6, on Camelback Road, Scottsdale, Arizona in room 24 was the place within the Matrix where Rocket told his sweetheart Mona to meet him to say goodbye before he left the Matrix again. After telling her about the Matrix and his plans to free her from it, and the telephone started ringing for his exit, Agent Brown (who had been tracking Rocket) took over Mona's body, turning it into a copy of his own and killed Rocket before he could escape.
