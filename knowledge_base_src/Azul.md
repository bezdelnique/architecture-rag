Azul was a male Redpill who along with Casque and Andromeda, was one of Anome's lieutenants who helped Anome with stealing the Cheat vials from Niobe and the Zionites. He then became a member of the renegade group known as Unlimit. It was believed that Azul turned from his organization and joined the fight against them when he killed Genev and Casque.
