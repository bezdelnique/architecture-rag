Charlie was a Bluepill, formerly a policeman for 28 years.

He worked as a security guard in the Grau Industries building and used to sleep on his post, although frequently insisting he didn't. He tried to dissuade his colleague, Pete, from joining the police force, warning about the worthless and unrewarding sacrifice it entailed.

His fate was unknown after he was immediately possessed by an Agent at work one evening, following the break in by an unknown Redpill who used explosives to enter the building.
