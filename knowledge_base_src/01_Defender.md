## Armada

Creature
Fleet

### Purpose

Launch Tow Bombs, defend 01

## Appearance

### Height

Possibly 100m

### Eye color

Red

The Armada (also referred to by Councilor West as the "Defense System") was a fleet of colossal floating Machines that formed a defensive ring at the base of the mountains surrounding the Machine City, 01. Insectile and crab-like in appearance, they were armed with multiple tube-fed cannons that fired bursts of Tow Bombs.

By the end of the Machine War, the Armada appeared to ring the entire perimeter of 01, apparently in a dormant, cocoon-like state. When a threat was detected, they deployed multiple weapon systems and appendages in preparation for enemy contact.

Neo and Trinity confronted the Armada as they flew the Logos to 01. The Armada, acting in coordination, immediately launched a devastating barrage of tow bombs at the Logos. Due to his connection to the Source, Neo was able to prematurely detonate a large number of the bombs. However, their sheer number and a squad of Sentinels attacking from the rear overwhelmed Neo's powers, forcing Trinity to fly above the permanent cloud cover to disengage and coast over the Armada into 01.

In The Matrix Resurrections, units of the Armada were deployed in the ongoing Machine Civil War.
