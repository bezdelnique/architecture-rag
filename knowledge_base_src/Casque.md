Casque was a male Redpill who along with Azul and Andromeda, was one of Anome's lieutenants who helped Anome with stealing the Cheat vials from Niobe and the Zionites. He then became a member of the renegade group known as Unlimit. Caque was killed along with Genev with the help of Plizkin, Mobu, Aquatium, and the turncoat Azul.

"Casque" means "helmet in French and Spanish, noting his red cap.
