## Description

Octacles has four wings and white armor.

## History

### The Matrix Resurrections (2021)

Octacles removes Neo's plugs from his body after he escapes his pod (for a second time). Octacles was accompanied by Cybebe, who flew Neo and themselves to the Mnemosyne. Octacles is introduced to Neo later in the Mnemosyne by Bugs.

## Trivia

Octacles' design is based off of fireflies or beetles.
Octacles' first appearance in The Matrix Resurrections (2021) is a callback to the scene in The Matrix (1999) where a Docbot frees Neo from his plugs when he wakes initially.
