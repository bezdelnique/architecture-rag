Lexy is a character in The Matrix Resurrections and an ally of Bugs.

Lexy is a loyal crewmember of the Mnemosyne, but she doesn't particularly believe in The One as a concept (though she still wants to find Neo the man). Instead, Lexy reveals that she actually idolizes Trinity and her legacy as one of Zion's greatest warriors even sixty years after her death. Lexy says she always wished she could be as powerful and fearless as Trinity was.

## Notes

Lexy has the same tribal tattoos along her torso in the Real world as she does in her Matrix self image. It's unclear whether this is a case of her avatar reflecting her real-life appearance, or if it was the other way around - that in real life she actively started trying to match the appearance of her Matrix avatar. Many people outside the Matrix look similar to their avatars, but don't have luxuries like jewelry, hair product, cosmetics, good clothing, etc. She has a tattoo on the back of her neck that looks somewhat like a plug socket with a red oval-like line and a black oval-like line. This likely represents her real-life plug on the back of her neck in the Matrix.
Multiple reviewers noted that there seems to be some implication that Bugs and Lexy have a more than platonic relationship, based on how Bugs rushes to hug Lexy when she's injured, lingering stares, etc. So far there hasn't been a cited interview in which the cast or Lana Wachowski have confirmed if this was intentional and if they played it as such.[1]
