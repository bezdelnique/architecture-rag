BlazinWolf was a trigger-happy Machinist operative in the Recursion instance of The Matrix Online.

Whilst Machinist operatives were tracking down and eliminating a dangerous escaped lupine, Vulg, he killed the exile informant, Rosaleen when she was startled by the sudden approach by one of the The Twins.
