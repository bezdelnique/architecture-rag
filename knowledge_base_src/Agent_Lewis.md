Agent Lewis was an undercover Agent in the comic Get It? who was posing as an unnamed friend to James, actually a fellow undercover named Agent Davis, and to Johnny, the human target. Agents Lewis and Davis watched The Matrix movie with Johnny and, after determining how little he really knew of the truth, allowed him to continue living his life, ignorant of his friends true identities.
