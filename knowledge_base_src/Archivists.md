Archivists are a group of renegade Exiles who are the masters of the Archive.

The Archivists defected from the Machines and sell out the services of the Archive to anyone, maintaining a strictly neutral position in the cold war following the Truce. However, when it comes to the protection of the Archive and its secrets, the Archivists enact a paranoid and fierce agenda.

## Archivist Proxy

Archivist Proxys are the Archivists that are able to teleport players into archive constructs. For the chosen archive, an Archivist Proxy requires the appropriate construct key from the qualified player who is also required to achieve other prior requirements to access the construct.
