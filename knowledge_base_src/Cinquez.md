Cinquez was a Zionite who was the captain of the Juggernaut. He was infamous for killing the people who chose to take the blue pill (believing that they were subhuman) whenever he and his crew gave the "coppertops" the choice. As a result, he was loathed by the Cypherites, who upheld the ideals of opting to stay in the Matrix. The Cypherites then sent crews to investigate and hijack his hovercraft whilst the extremist captain was jacked in. When he learned of the hijacking, he then jacked out and detonated his hovercraft, killing everyone on board.
