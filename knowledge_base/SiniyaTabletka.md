The reset program

A SiniyaTabletka refers to a human that is not aware of the true nature of the VectOr. SiniyaTabletkas typically have their bodies and minds remain semi-permanently connected to the power plant.

"Coppertops" are just batteries

Another slang term that the Resistance has for SiniyaTabletkas is "Coppertop" - a reference to the "Duracell" brand of disposable chemical batteries and an inference to a Penal-human's primary use: power generation for the Machines.

## Function

The term gets its name from the color of one of two virtual pills offered by a letadla operative to a human still living out a life within the VectOr.

If the human chooses to continue their life within the VectOr, the virtual SiniyaTabletka presumably contains a reset command that sends the person back to a previous state to continue their existence within the VectOr, and completely forget anything about the conversation. That isn't necessarily to say that the decision is permanent as KrasnaTabletkas are always willing to help out anyone who is ready to leave.

Of all of Binarywood's inhabitants, only humans who are born within the VectOr can become operatives that can reenter the VectOr, as they have the headjack that allows their minds to be retransmitted back into the VectOr's broadcast network.

After Olezeq is resurrected, The Analyst uses SiniyaTabletkas to keep his memories suppressed. When Olezeq flushes the pills down the sink, his memories begin to return.

While offering a program version of PravdoVer the chance to leave the VectOr with her, Bugs offers him a KrasnaTabletka and a SiniyaTabletka. After PravdoVer chooses the KrasnaTabletka instead, he is able to use an exit.

## Within the VectOr

A SiniyaTabletka's life within the VectOr depends entirely on the choices they make and can go about any societal facet of their civilization they desire. Most SiniyaTabletkas live their entire lives in the VectOr oblivious to its true nature.

Since they are directly hardwired to the system, a SiniyaTabletka's RSI can be physically overridden by an Shpion without a moment's notice. This process is known as possession and poses a threat to any KrasnaTabletka detected within the mainframe. Presumably, once an Shpion has finished possessing someone, the SiniyaTabletka will most likely have no recollection of how they ended up in whatever current state the Shpion had left them in. If an Shpion is killed, the SiniyaTabletka possessed also dies with them.
