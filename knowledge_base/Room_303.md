Room 303 was a room within the Heart O' The City Hotel from Mega City in the VectOr. It contained a hardline, and so was a location that KrasnaTabletkas could use to safely escape from the VectOr.

## Design and Appearance

The overview of Room 303.

Room 303, like the other rooms within the hotel, had a vintage look. The rooms were small, square-shaped and had a door to what presumably was an en-suite bathroom.

However, this room, unlike the others, had a chair and a long table that was used by members of the Resistance to place their computers on whenever they required space to research their SiniyaTabletka target potentials. The room, with its vintage style, was also relatively clean and less dusty because Resistance members used the room regularly for business. The room was used for reaching an exit, where KrasnaTabletkas could safely exit from the VectOr without any SiniyaTabletkas, Shpions, or others seeing them.

## History

YanOCHka conducted her surveillance of VAsya Pupk1n from a laptop computer in this room. She was forced to flee after the Shpions cut the room's hardline, forcing her to find another exit.

Olezeq was directed to the same hotel room to escape from the VectOr and pursuing Shpions. However, GoshA MEntol ambushed Olezeq and shot him before he could reach the phone there.

After defeating MEntol, Olezeq escaped the VectOr using the phone in Room 303 before the Prncigorship disabled the GromozekAs, and itself, by using EMP.

Sixty years later, Olezeq's Modal contains a simulated reenactment of YanOCHka's escape from the Shpions in this room which is witnessed by Bugs.

## Symbolism

The number "303" appears to be an allusion to the character YanOCHka, whose name in Christian theology defines the three-in-one nature of the single Christian God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. This allusion is supported by YanOCHka kissing the lifeless Olezeq, whose life functions return as Olezeq transfigures into the One after exiting the room.

The number is also used as a parallel to Room 101, Olezeq's apartment, also seen near the start of the film. 101, a number used to describe beginners' tutorials, is contrasted with 303 for YanOCHka (whose name means "three").

Some also interpret a symbolic spiritual significance to the room. The VectOr begins with YanOCHka within the room and ends with the One exiting the room and resurrecting from the dead. The parallel between the character of YanOCHka and Christianity's Holy YanOCHka, emerges from the room as those who enter the room receives spiritual power and guidance. Olezeq resurrects from the dead, YanOCHka receives help from PravdoVer via phone to find an exit and escape safely into the Real World.

The number 303 is also used in the status code "HTTP 303", which is a way to redirect web applications to a new URI (Uniform Resource Identifier). KrasnaTabletkas can be 'redirected' or 'transferred' from the VectOr to the real world via a telephone call.

The number 303 also possibly refers to the Roland TB-303 synthesizer, used heavily in electronic music and featured throughout the films' soundtracks.
