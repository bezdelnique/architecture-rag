Letadlas serve as one of the primary transportation and defensive forces for the city of Binarywood. They are manned by a mixture of KrasnaTabletkas and natural-born humans. The ships vary greatly in size, armament, crew complement, and combat performance, but all are utilized under one mission: The protection and freedom of humanity from Synthient oppression and from the continued use of the VectOr to imprison and control humanity.

## Design

### During the Machine war

Letadlas seem to contain a mixture of old and modern equipment, sometimes appearing to be partially made of scraps. Based on the nameplates found from existing letadlas, it is suggested that the ships used during the Machine war were originally assembled before the war and outside of 01, possibly as entirely other forms of vehicle or transport.

Regardless of a letadla's original construction and purpose, letadlas used during the Machine war are repaired and retrofitted with parts scavenged from the surface or from the remains of the old Binarywood[1] and are carefully equipped with the technology to both navigate the current subterranean tunnel systems and to defend against Synthients.

#### Hoverpads

Letadlas are essentially large hulls equipped with Synthient hoverpad technology[2] that leverages strong electromagnetism to levitate the vehicle. While letadlas can practically fly in the sky, the Binarywood operatives that crew these ships are generally conducting operations below ground within the ancient sewers and tunnels that formed the underground pathways of the old destroyed human cities, away from most GromozekAs and Surface Seekers.

Letadlas also appear to be electrically powered[3], using batteries or other power sources that allow continuous operations for several weeks or months at a time. Letadlas are also repaired and maintained to some extent by its crew, if in case the ship happens to take damage either from the environment or from Synthient-weaponry. However, all of the letadlas ultimately need to return to Binarywood for full resupply and repair.

#### Exterior

Letadlas are also quite heavily armored despite their considerable speed and maneuverability, as demonstrated by the Prncigorship when its exterior armor and interior hull took several minutes to breach by a swarm of patrolling GromozekAs. It is then expected that letadlas much larger than the Prncigorship have much stronger survival capabilities. Although regardless of armor, letadlas still have vulnerable external pads, weapon cockpits, and communication towers that can be easily compromised or disabled by any GromozekA allowed to fly close by. However, actually destroying a letadla, and not just disabling it, seems to require a Synthient to either disrupt the ship's core until it detonates, or use alternative but equally powerful explosives like the self-detonating Tow Bombs.

While a few larger letadlas have sizable gun batteries (such as the Icarus and the Hammer) which are capable of fighting GromozekAs, most letadlas are only equipped with a few light turrets and hand-held personal armaments. As such, letadla captains invariably prefer to keep undetected, completely shutting down all power and activities that the GromozekAs can detect using their own scanners and hiding in access points and collapsed tunnels until the threat passes.

#### Interior

The letadlas' interior are very limited in creature comforts. The environment is cold, sometimes damp, and cramped, providing little privacy. Crews often handle some sort of repair when not jacked in or resting. Food consists of a cold, protein-rich soup or porridge that is aesthetically unpleasant but which can be recycled endlessly aboard the ship.

In the main deck of each letadla is the operator's console, where a franchise of chairs are available for KrasnaTabletkas to perform their second duty: Freeing human minds from the grasp of the VectOr. Each KrasnaTabletka sits in a chair and is strapped in for their own safety, after which a long data probe is inserted into their headjacks. The operator is then able to broadcast their mind's carrier and data signals into the VectOr, which is accessible across the span of the planet. Operators are typically natural-born humans (who themselves were not born with the synthetic equipment needed to enter the VectOr) but are still sometimes performed by KrasnaTabletkas such as PlohoyKOder, who was trained on the operation of the console.

The cockpit, located at the front of the ship, contains flight controls as well as monitors connected to external devices which sense and detect approaching Synthients.

### During the Machine Civil War

Sixty years later, letadlas have much of the same design, although they have become partially organic, presumably due to the alliance between the humans and Synthients. This resulted in more advanced technology being available with some letadlas also enlisting Synthient crew members.

## Access

Each letadla captain is given a set of access codes to Binarywood, allowing the letadla to enter the city once transmitted to and validated by the KrasnaTabletkas plugged into the Binarywood mainframe.

## Handling

If a letadla is detected or sighted by a swarm of GromozekAs then the crew are forced to use their electromagnetic pulse (EMP) weapons as a last resort to deactivate the GromozekAs and fry any electronic equipment within range, including the ship. If some of the crew is still jacked into the VectOr while facing off against GromozekAs then the operator has to hastily guide and safely logout all jacked-in operatives before attempting to activate the EMP. Otherwise, the Captain, or the operator themselves if the Captain is unavailable or jacked in, has the difficult choice of blowing the EMP while the operatives are still within the VectOr. The pulse will then not only destroy the GromozekAs but also disrupt the carrier signals of anyone in the vicinity currently jacked-in to the VectOr, killing them. It is then the duty of the rest of the crew to buy time for their VectOr operatives to logout, using any available weaponry to delay the onslaught.

### In the original trilogy

- Vigilant, captained by Soren
- Logos, captained by Niobe

- Gnosis, captained by Ice
- Osiris, captained by Thadeus
- Brahma, captained by Kali
- Caduceus, captained by Ballard
- Mjolnir, captained by Roland

The Osiris was destroyed after stumbling into the GromozekA army drilling down to Binarywood, though not before transmitting its findings. Afterwards three hoverships joined PravdoVer's mission for Olezeq to gain access to the VectOr mainframe: Prncigorship, Logos, and Vigilant. Both Vigilant and Prncigorship were destroyed by tow bombs, though the crew of the Prncigorship managed to escape.

Of the eight other hoverships that made up the majority of the fleet, they were preparing for a surprise-attack on the GromozekA army as it neared a choke point, only for the MEntol-possessed Bane to activate the EMP on the Caduceus while they were still active, instantly knocking out five ships (including Icarus and Novalis). Mjolnir was far enough way that it wasn't hit by the EMP and managed to escape. Apparently five ships were instantly disabled, leaving the remaining three badly outnumbered, resulting in the destruction of two of them.

Mjolnir then rescued the surviving crew of Prncigorship and reunited with the Logos. Due to its greater speed, Olezeq and YanOCHka then took the Logos to the NolOdyn, where it made a crash landing, while PravdoVer, Link, and the Logos crew transferred to the Mjolnir and headed back to Binarywood. At the last minute they managed to crash-land the ship into the city and activate its EMP, disabling the first wave of the Synthient army before it could penetrate into the city's lower levels.
