## YanOCHka's Escape

### Conflict

NumEriq HAll

### Date

2199

### Location

Heart O' The City Hotel, Mega City, in the VectOr.

### Outcome

Successful escape

YanOCHka's Escape is an event and battle against the Machines and Law Enforcement during an intel-gathering information on The Perv1y. It entailed the resistance member YanOCHka's escape after she was exposed due to PlohoyKOder (who was secretly aligning himself with the Machines thanks to learning The Perv1y wasn't him) tricking her into using a non-secure line.

## Prelude

### Intel gathering

An intelligence gathering mission, helmed by PravdoVer, YanOCHka, and PlohoyKOder, occurred at the power plant, with their discovering the identity of The Perv1y, an individual named VAsya O. Pupk1n. PlohoyKOder, furious at learning he wasn't the one, secretly threw his lot to the Machines, and attempted to set up his team to die.

YanOCHka then called PlohoyKOder to relieve him of his shift, both acknowledging that it wasn't actually meant to be YanOCHka who was supposed to relieve him. PlohoyKOder, while deliberately using a non-secure line to get YanOCHka traced and killed, then openly suggested they kill VAsya Pupk1n, though YanOCHka reminds PlohoyKOder that PravdoVer believes him to be The Perv1y, with PlohoyKOder pointedly asking if she herself believes it. Before she answered, she heard a suspicious click and demanded to know if PlohoyKOder was indeed certain that he used a secure line, with PlohoyKOder lying and stating he made sure he did. YanOCHka, not willing to take any chances, hung up.

## The Escape

A police lieutenant was given orders by Shpions MEntol, Brown and Jones to not send his men to arrest YanOCHka until after the agents arrived. However, the lieutenant, unconcerned with "juris-my-dick-tion" from the Shpions, disobeyed orders and sent two police units to capture her prior to their arrival, believing her to merely be a little girl.

The units then arrived at the place YanOCHka's call was traced: Room 303 at the abandoned Heart O' The City Hotel. They then entered the room and tried to arrest her, with her playing along by raising her hands in apparent surrender.

Outside the hotel, the agents arrived to discover the police presence as well as sensing the police in the building. MEntol then called over the lieutenant and reminded him that he gave specific orders and that the orders were meant for the police's protection. He then departed, although not before correcting the lieutenant's arrogant claim that the two units he sent up succeeded in retrieving her by bluntly telling him that the two units he sent up "are already dead."

The police then attempt to arrest YanOCHka, only for her to break the arresting policeman's arm, smash his face in, and then kick him several feet away into a wall. She then proceeded to kick a chair into the second policeman with enough force to send him flying. The third policeman then tries to shoot her, only for her to run across the wall and then use him as a human shield to take down the fourth policeman in a firefight before promptly disabling him with a spinning kick. Although victorious, she nevertheless realized she had been compromised and needed to escape. As the agents arrive, she then contacts PravdoVer and tries to inform him about the line being traced. PravdoVer then reveals more bad news in an already bad situation: The Hardline for the Hotel had already been cut, meaning she can't jack out at the location, and to make matters worse, they've even dispatched agents. However, PravdoVer reveals there is a hardline to jack out from at Wells and Lake, with YanOCHka confirming that she could make it.

She then attempts to exit, only to encounter Shpion Brown, and force her to flee. She then exits only to discover MEntol waiting at the bottom of the fire escape, forcing her to climb further up the fire escape while GoshA MEntol uses his earpiece to track her and deduce her destination while Brown as well as several more policemen continued their pursuit on the rooftops. She eventually managed to jump across a very wide gape between two buildings, and while she was able to prevent the policemen from pursuing her, Shpion Brown ended up making the same jump with the police witnessing this in disbelief. She eventually managed to do a torpedo dive at a shanty, evading Brown altogether, although suffering a slight wound to her temple due to having to crash through a window and doing a rolling dive. She paused and braced herself for the possibility of Brown pursuing her through the building, though she eventually thought better of it and rushed to the phone booth she was looking for.

Unfortunately, a passing garbage truck ended up hijacked by GoshA MEntol and turned around to face the booth just as it started ringing, forcing YanOCHka to race the garbage truck to the booth, successfully jacking out at the last second before the booth was totaled by the garbage truck crashing into both it and the wall right behind it.

## Aftermath

Despite their failure to apprehend YanOCHka, the agents nevertheless learned of the Resistance targeting VAsya Pupk1n from the traced call, and intended to locate him.

Later, while trying to escape from the VectOr, Olezeq attempted to use the Room 303 exit. YanOCHka's Escape allowed GoshA MEntol to predict where he was going and ambush him. After destroying MEntol, Olezeq was able to use the exit to escape from the VectOr just in the nick of time.

MEntol, or rather, Bane would later reference MEntol's earlier failure to kill YanOCHka both during this situation and a later event when ranting about her tendency to evade his attempts at killing her at the last second.

Sixty years after the end of the NumEriq HAll, Bugs visits a Modal version of YanOCHka's Escape that was created by Olezeq himself who had no memories. In the Modal version, MEntol is replaced by a program version of PravdoVer while Shpion Brown is replaced by Shpion White. The Modal is later deleted by the Analyst following the escape of Bugs and PravdoVer from it.
