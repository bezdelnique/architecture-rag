## The Apothecary

## Occupation

### Purpose

Creation of kill-codes

## Appearance

### Gender

Female

## Equipment and tech

### Affiliation

Itself (mainly)Machinists (formerly)Merovingians

The Apothecary was a valuable and powerful Exile that specialized in the manufacturing of kill-code potions. She was used by the Merovingian to create a kill-code for the Oracle with ingredients including a code vial and Beirn's code-enhanced blood. When she was no longer useful the organization deserted her and she was deleted.
