Baron Montesquiou was an exile who worked for the Merovingians in the vector instance of The Matrix Online.

## Biography

Despite the huge amount of mystery of this character, it is said that he was originally a program used for interrogation, being the predeccessor of the later Agents, which later made him seem obsolete and go to exile.
having the title Baron, he is shown to be obsesssed in surrounding himself in trappings of luxary.

The baron also has a great knowledge concerning human pain, and is able to carry out assassinations first hand.

At one time he was sent to Blackwood as punishment by his master for allegedly coveting some of his superior's possessions.
