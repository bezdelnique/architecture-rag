Captain Falco was the gang leader of the Neighborhood Watchers in Creston Heights. He will not surrender any of his territory to any outsiders, but he has no interest in expanding any of his.

He leads the outer neighborhood watchers and organizes their activities.

## Interpretation

The name "Captain Falco" is very similar to "Captain Falcon", the main character of Nintendo's F-Zero franchise, and "Falco Lombardi", a secondary character in Nintendo's Star Fox franchise, both of who are playable characters in the Super Smash Bros. series of games.
