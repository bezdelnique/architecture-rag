The Antediluvian (also given the alias Dr. Voss) was an exile program that was once the right-hand hand of the The Merovingian. He was however replaced by Flood who was believed to have killed the former. He secretly began to plot for revenge with the The Boxmaker. Both exiles were the twin antagonists of the Pandora's Box live event in The Matrix Online.

## Bio

At some point in the past, The Antediluvian was The Merovingian's right hand man. After failing The Merovingian, he was replaced, and killed, by Flood. Despite both Flood and The Merovingian believing that the exile was dead. He survived and secretly began to plot his revenge.

At some other later time, he formed an alliance with The Boxmaker, who blamed the Merv for his brother, The Keymaker.

The Antediluvian then posed as Dr. Voss, becoming a scientist at Kalt Chemical Engineering.

## Skills and Character

This program was able to stave off death despite his apparent execution. When battling the player, he made use of his own Simulacrum. He was not above working with others, as shown when he allied with The Boxmaker.
