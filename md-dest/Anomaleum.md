The Anomaleum is the location in The Matrix Resurrections where a resurrected Neo and Trinity were kept isolated and jacked into the Matrix after the events of the original The Matrix trilogy.

## History

Rama Kandra made the original plans for the Anomaleum for The Analyst but gave the plans to his daughter, Sati, before being deleted. The original purpose of the tower was to resurrect Neo and Trinity in order to study the human race.

After being freed from the tower by the Mnemosyne's crew, Neo and the crew later returns to free Trinity from the Matrix with Sati leading the rescue mission.

## Appearances

The Anomaluem was first seen in the The Matrix Resurrections when Neo wakes from the Matrix for a second time and before being taken to the Mnemosyne by Octacles and Cybebe.
